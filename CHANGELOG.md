# Changelog

Notable changes to Signal Engine are recorded here. Version labels describe the source state, not independently certified investment performance or live-deployment readiness.

## 3.4 — 2026-10-22

- Added strategy-specific structural stop/target hypotheses, opposing-structure reward checks and condition-level rejection evidence.
- Improved complete-universe scan reporting and differentiated evaluated, skipped, failed and stale market states.
- Added signal expiry for old actionable proposals while retaining historical audit records.
- Reworked Trade Journey with price-based entry/stop/target distances and an interactive observation chart.
- Restored best-effort host, Docker and EC2 information in System Health; refined public methodology, admin details, scrolling, light/dark surfaces and interaction accessibility.
- Standardized repository documentation, contribution/security guidance and release verification reporting.

## 3.3 — 2026-10-22

- Introduced the calm studio interface and restored the original three-bar identity across public/admin interfaces and icons.

## 3.2 — 2026-10-22

- Introduced animated editorial concepts for the public site and operator workspace; later superseded by the 3.3 visual direction.

## 3.1 — 2026-10-22

- Unified four strategies in the primary scanning pipeline with shared arbitration, risk governance, persistent strategy controls and a combined Strategy Lab.

## 3.0 — 2026-10-22

- Introduced the four research strategy implementations, research ledger, Binance-only positioning intelligence and Groq model fallback.

For verification boundaries of the distributed 3.4 source, see [docs/VALIDATION.md](docs/VALIDATION.md). Historical version-by-version prose reports have been removed to avoid competing operational instructions.
